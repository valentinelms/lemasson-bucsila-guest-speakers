        Epilogue

        What we'll take with us.

        The first speakers of the semester were younger than us, and they still ran the room. What stayed with us is less the tools than the habit behind them: describe clearly, build fast, put it out there. And say yes when a door opens, because one breakfast conversation is how this whole session happened.
